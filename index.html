    Club access

    Enter your name and email address and the app will send a secure sign-in link. New club representatives must be approved by the administrator before they can add dates.

    Your name is shown only to the calendar administrator and will not appear on the public calendar.

    To use another phone, tablet or computer, enter the same details on that device and open its new sign-in link. Your existing approval will carry across.

    Subscribe to the live calendar

    Subscribe once and published date additions, changes and cancellations will be picked up automatically by your calendar provider.

    Google Calendar

    Copy the link. On a computer, open Google Calendar and choose Other calendars → + → From URL, then paste it.

    Apple Calendar or iPhone

    Use “Subscribe on this device” below. If asked, confirm that you want to subscribe rather than import a single file.

    Update timing

    The feed changes as soon as an approved date is saved, but Google and Apple decide how often to check it. Updates may take several hours to appear.
